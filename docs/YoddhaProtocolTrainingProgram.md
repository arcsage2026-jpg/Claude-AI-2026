# The Yoddha Protocol
### A 12-Week Integrated Training Program
**Shaolin Kung Fu · Yoga & Surya / Chandra Namaskar · Hindu Squats & Dand · Pull-ups · Military Calisthenics · Tai Chi**

Built to develop all five qualities together — **strength, cardio, flexibility, stamina, endurance** — using bodyweight and traditional practice only, with **Shaolin Kung Fu, yoga, and pehlwani conditioning at its core**. No gym required.

---

## How this program is built (read first)

**Assumptions I made** (scale as needed — see notes at the end):
- **6 training days + 1 rest day** per week
- **~60–75 min per session** (can be compressed to 40 min)
- You're starting from a **beginner–intermediate** base and progressing to advanced over 12 weeks
- No current injuries. If you have any, skip the aggravating movement and substitute.

**The logic:** each modality is placed on the day where it does the most good, and no push/pull/leg pattern is trained hard two days in a row. So recovery is built into the split itself.

**The structure:** The *weekly split* tells you **what** to do each day. The *progression tables* tell you **how much** to do based on which phase you're in.

| Phase | Weeks | Theme |
|-------|-------|-------|
| **Phase 1 — Foundation** | 1–4 | Build technique, base strength, breath-movement link |
| **Phase 2 — Build** | 5–8 | Add volume, speed, and load; introduce inversions |
| **Phase 3 — Advanced** | 9–12 | High volume, skill work (arm balances, muscle-ups), conditioning peaks |

> **Deload:** In Week 4, 8, and 12, cut all volume by ~40% for the whole week. This is when your body actually adapts. Don't skip it.

## Universal Warm-up (8–10 min, every session)

1. **Sukshma Vyayama** — joint rotations top to bottom: neck, shoulders, wrists, waist, hips, knees, ankles (10 each direction). Extra wrist prep on Dand and arm-balance days.
2. **2–3 slow rounds of Surya Namaskar** to build internal heat (these are separate from your working rounds).

## Universal Cool-down (5–10 min, every session)

1. **Static stretches** for whatever you trained (hamstrings, hips, shoulders, spine).
2. **Pranayama** — Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril) 5 min, then Bhramari 5 rounds.
3. **Shavasana** 3–5 min. Non-negotiable on hard days.

## 1. Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutation) — cardio · flexibility · full-body warmth

**The 12-count sequence (one round):**
1. Pranamasana (prayer) → 2. Hasta Uttanasana (raised arms) → 3. Uttanasana (forward fold) → 4. Ashwa Sanchalanasana (equestrian, right leg back) → 5. Phalakasana (plank) → 6. Ashtanga Namaskara (eight-limbed) → 7. Bhujangasana (cobra) → 8. Adho Mukha Svanasana (down dog) → 9. Ashwa Sanchalanasana (left leg forward) → 10. Uttanasana → 11. Hasta Uttanasana → 12. Pranamasana.

*Switch the lead leg each round.* Inhale on expansion, exhale on folds.

| | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|--|--|--|--|
| **Warm-up pace** | 3–5 rounds slow | 8–10 rounds | 12–15 rounds |
| **Tue fast rounds** | 5 rounds brisk | 10 rounds fast | 15 rounds fast (cardio driver) |
| **Sat long set** | 8 rounds | 12–15 rounds | 20–24 rounds continuous |

The cooling counterpart to Surya. 14 positions built around **Ardha Chandrasana** (crescent-moon lunges) on each side. Done Wednesday and evenings — it lengthens the hip flexors and side body that all the pushing and running tightens up.

| **Rounds** | 2–4 slow | 4–6 | 6–8 + deeper crescent holds (15–20s each side) |

## 3. Hanuman Dand — strength (chest, shoulders, triceps, core) + spinal mobility

The pehlwani wrestler's push-up — also known as the **Hindu push-up**, and a cornerstone of the Shaolin Kung Fu block. *(Note: this is the flowing "dand," not Hanumanasana the splits pose — that lives in the yoga section.)*

**Technique — it's a wave, not a plank push-up:**
1. Start in an inverted-V, hips high (down-dog shape), feet a bit wider than a normal push-up.
2. **Swoop** the chest down and forward, skimming close to the floor (like a dive-bomber).
3. Continue up into a **cobra/upward-dog arch**, hips low, chest and chin lifted.
4. Reverse the wave — push hips back up to the inverted-V start. That's one rep.

Breathe out on the swoop down, in on the rise into cobra.

| | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|--|--|--|--|
| **Volume** | 3 × 6–8 (knees down if needed) | 4 × 12–15 full range | 5 × 20–25 |
| **Progression** | control the tempo | full flow, no pause | feet elevated / clap variation / one flowing set of 40 |

## 4. Pull-ups — strength (back, biceps, grip) · endurance

Follow the ladder. **Only move up a level when the current one feels controlled with 1–2 reps in reserve.**

| Level | Movement | Target |
|-------|----------|--------|
| **0** | Dead hang (grip + shoulders) | accumulate 30–60s |
| **1** | Scapular pulls + negatives (jump up, lower 3–5s) | 4 × 3–5 negatives |
| **2** | Band-assisted pull-ups | 4 × 5 |
| **3** | Strict full pull-ups | build 3×5 → 3×8–10 |
| **4 (adv.)** | Weighted · archer · typewriter · L-sit · commando · → **muscle-up** | skill-dependent |

| | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|--|--|--|--|
| **Thursday work** | Dead hangs 5×20–30s + negatives 4×3 | Band/strict 4×3–5 + hangs | Strict 5×6–10, then add weight/archer |

*Rotate grips across weeks: pronated (pull-up), supinated (chin-up), neutral, wide.*

## 5. Military Calisthenics — strength · cardio · stamina · endurance

**Exercise bank:**
- **Push:** push-ups (standard, diamond, wide, decline), dips
- **Legs:** air squats, jump squats, lunges, walking lunges, calf raises
- **Core:** sit-ups, flutter kicks, leg raises, plank, hollow hold, Russian twists
- **Conditioning:** burpees, mountain climbers, bear crawls, high knees
- **Cardio:** steady runs, interval sprints, shuttle runs, rucking (loaded backpack walk)

**Formats:** circuits (Tue/Fri), AMRAP/EMOM, and PT pyramids as you advance.

| Exercise | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|----------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Push-ups** | 4 × 10 | 5 × 15 | 6 × 20+ / weighted / pyramids |
| **Squats** | 4 × 15 | 4 × 25 | 5 × 30 + jump squats |
| **Plank** | 3 × 30s | 4 × 45s | 5 × 60s + hollow holds |
| **Burpees** | 3 × 8 | 4 × 12 | 5 × 15–20 |
| **Run** | 2 km steady / 6×200m | 3–4 km / 8×200m | 5 km / sprint ladders / ruck |

**Sample Tuesday circuit** (repeat rounds per phase — 2 / 3 / 4 rounds): 15 squats → 10 push-ups → 10 burpees → 20 mountain climbers → 30s plank → 90s run/rest. Then finish with fast Surya rounds.

## 7. Yoga Asana Ladder — flexibility · strength · balance

Trained mainly **Wednesday** (flexibility) and **Friday** (strength holds & arm balances). Hold times grow by phase: **20–30s → 30–45s → 45–60s.**

**Phase 1 — Beginner:** Tadasana, Vrikshasana (tree), Trikonasana, Virabhadrasana I & II (warrior), Baddha Konasana, Paschimottanasana, Bhujangasana, Marjariasana (cat-cow), Setu Bandhasana (bridge), Balasana, Shavasana.

**Phase 2 — Intermediate:** Utkatasana (chair), Parsvakonasana, Ardha Chandrasana, Navasana (boat), Dhanurasana (bow), Ustrasana (camel), Salamba Sarvangasana (shoulder stand), Halasana (plough), Ardha Matsyendrasana (twist).

**Phase 3 — Advanced:** Sirsasana (headstand), Bakasana (crow), Pincha Mayurasana (forearm stand), Adho Mukha Vrikshasana (handstand at wall), Hanumanasana (front splits), Eka Pada Rajakapotasana (king pigeon), Chakrasana (wheel), Vrischikasana (scorpion).

> Always warm the neck and shoulders thoroughly before any inversion (Sirsasana / Sarvangasana). Build inversions against a wall first.

## 8. Shaolin Kung Fu — the core discipline — strength · endurance · coordination

The spine of the program, trained **3× per week (Mon · Thu · Sat)**. These are the no-weight methods Shaolin monks, pehlwani wrestlers, and martial artists used to build strength that holds up under fatigue — functional, not mirror muscle. Two halves: **dynamic methods** (rhythmic, flowing) and **isometric holds** (static, tendon-building).

### Dynamic methods

| Method | Builds | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|--------|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Hindu push-up / Dand** | Chest, shoulders, spine | 3 × 6–8 | 4 × 12–15 | 5 × 20–25 |
| **Hindu squat (Baithak)** | Legs, lungs, endurance | 3 × 20 | 4 × 40 | 5 × 60–100 |
| **Bear crawl** | Shoulders, core, grip | 3 × 10m | 4 × 15m | 5 × 20m fwd+back |
| **Bear hug walk** (loaded carry) | Grip, carry strength, stability | 3 × 20m light | 4 × 30m moderate | 5 × 40m heavy |
| **Stance work** (Ma Bu · Gong Bu) | Legs, hips, rooting | hold 30s | 45–60s + transitions | 90s + stance changes |
| **Shaolin kicks** (front · crescent) | Active flexibility, hips | 2 × 10 / side | 3 × 10 | 3 × 12 + height |

### The 25–30 minute Shaolin routine (Saturday core)

Run as a circuit — **2 rounds (P1) → 3 (P2) → 4 (P3)**, minimal rest, quality over speed:

1. **Hindu squats** — 20 / 40 / 60 reps
2. **Hindu push-ups (Dand)** — 8 / 15 / 25 reps
3. **Bear crawl** — 10 / 15 / 20 m forward + back
4. **Bear hug walk** — 20 / 30 / 40 m (hug a sandbag, stone, or heavy pack)
5. **Horse stance hold** — 30 / 60 / 90 s to close each round

### Common mistakes to avoid

- **Hindu squat:** heels lifting or knees caving — keep it rhythmic, rise onto the balls of the feet deliberately, then plant the heels; breathe in a steady cadence.
- **Hindu push-up:** rushing the wave or skipping the cobra arch — move slowly through the full swoop-and-arch.
- **Bear crawl:** hips riding too high — knees an inch off the floor, back flat, opposite hand and foot moving together.
- **Bear hug walk:** rounding the back under load — chest tall, brace the core, short controlled steps.
- **Horse stance:** knees past the toes or heels rising — sit straight down, drive knees out, weight in the heels.

### Iron Body — isometric holds

The static complement. Run as **finishers after Mon & Thu**, or a standalone circuit. Breathe slow and steady; trembling is normal.

| Hold | Builds | Phase 1 | Phase 2 | Phase 3 |
|------|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| **Horse Stance (Ma Bu)** | Thighs, glutes, groin, core | 3 × 30s | 3 × 45–60s | 3 × 90s + arms extended |
| **Wall Sit** | Quads | 3 × 30s | 3 × 45s | 3 × 60–90s |
| **Iron Plank** (low push-up hold) | Chest, shoulders, triceps, core | 3 × 20s | 3 × 40s | 3 × 60s |
| **Fingertip / knuckle plank** | Fingers, wrists, forearms | 3 × 10–15s | 3 × 25s | 3 × 40s |
| **Isometric pull hold** | Back, biceps, grip | Dead hang 20–30s | Chin-over 15–20s | Chin-over 30s+ |
| **Hollow body hold** | Deep core | 3 × 20s | 3 × 30–40s | 3 × 60s |
| **One-leg stance (Jin Ji Du Li)** | Stabilizers, ankles, balance | 30s / leg | 60s / leg | 2–3 min / leg |

## 9. Beej Mantra — chakra seed sounds (meditation cool-down)

Seven seed (*bija*) sounds, one per chakra, chanted root → crown. Used to settle the nervous system after training — best on **Wednesday (flow day)** and at the end of any session, after pranayama.

**How to practise:** Sit tall (Sukhasana), spine long, eyes closed. Chant each bija aloud on a long, slow exhale, letting the vibration settle at that centre. Do 3, 7, or 11 rounds per sound, or one slow ascending pass. Keep it gentle — return to natural breathing if you feel light-headed.

| Bija | Chakra | Location | Element |
|------|--------|----------|---------|
| **LAM** | Muladhara · Root | Base of spine | Earth |
| **VAM** | Svadhisthana · Sacral | Below the navel | Water |
| **RAM** | Manipura · Solar plexus | Navel | Fire |
| **YAM** | Anahata · Heart | Centre of the chest | Air |
| **HUM** | Vishuddha · Throat | Throat | Ether |
| **OM** | Ajna · Third eye | Between the brows | Light |
| **AUM** | Sahasrara · Crown | Crown of the head | Consciousness |

*Note: some lineages use **HAM** (not HUM) for the throat, **OM** for the third eye, and leave the crown silent. The sequence above follows the set you're working with.*

## Safety, recovery & progression rules

- **Progress on control, not ego.** Move up only when the current volume feels clean with 1–2 reps in reserve.
- **Wrist care** matters — Dands, planks, and arm balances load the wrists. Warm them and build gradually.
- **Pain vs. discomfort:** effort-burn is fine; sharp or joint pain means stop and substitute.
- **Deload weeks (4, 8, 12):** ~40% less volume. This is where adaptation happens.
- **Breathe with the movement** — never hold your breath through a rep.
- **Recovery basics:** 7–8h sleep, protein with each meal, hydrate (more in Ahmedabad heat — favour early-morning or evening sessions in summer).

---

## How to scale this down

- **4 days/week:** keep Mon (Push), Thu (Pull), Fri (Full-body), Wed (Flow). Drop Tue & Sat, or fold a short run into Fri.
- **40-min sessions:** do warm-up → the day's *one* main block (e.g., just Hanuman Dand + core on Mon) → cooldown. Skip accessory circuits.
- **Very tight day:** 10 rounds Surya Namaskar + 5 min pranayama covers cardio, mobility, and breath in ~12 minutes.

*Cycle back through after Week 12 starting from the Phase 3 volumes as your new baseline — that's when advanced skill work (muscle-ups, handstand holds, full 24-form fluency, 24+ round Surya sets) becomes the focus.*
